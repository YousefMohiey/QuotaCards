Your gaming and streaming packages,
used on purpose.

QuotaVPN is a personal VPN built for ISPs that sell your line in quota buckets. What a connection counts against is decided from the TLS handshake, so QuotaVPN makes your traffic ride the bucket you pick: your gaming package, your streaming package, or general quota.

What it does

Counts against the package you choose

Gamerz and Streamerz profiles carry their own server list, so game and streaming traffic rides the package that should pay for it instead of your general quota.

Three transports, honestly labelled

Standard, gaming and WireGuard modes. Each one states in plain words what it spends from, because a faster mode that counts differently is a decision, not a detail.

English and Arabic, completely

The whole interface ships in both languages, with the layout keeping its shape. Nothing mirrors, nothing is half translated.

Updates itself

One installer, no admin needed, and the app keeps itself current from the releases page. The Android build sideloads once and updates the same way.

How it works

1

Pick a card

A card is a profile: a name, a domain to ride, and the transport you want to use.

2

Pick a server

Every card carries its own server list, plus any custom address you add yourself.

3

Connect

One tap. The session runs, the counters run, and closing the window keeps it alive in the tray.
